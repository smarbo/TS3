# V3 future sealed-test protocol

Frozen before any V3 future-period outcome is opened, 2026-10-02. The accepted 2026-09-27/28 Kraken tape and all V0–V2 smokes are **development** evidence. This protocol does not authorize a trading account, orders or V4 work.

## Freeze and collection

Before the first sealed raw record is inspected for outcomes, commit and hash: source/analysis code, `v3.features.1`, target and label versions, exact artifact, normalization/calibration state, logistic penalty, decision policy and cost assumptions, comparison scripts, primary metric and gates below. Record these in a signed-off manifest with the future run ID and UTC collection boundaries. Collect only public Kraken BTC/USD data through the accepted V0 recorder; require clean manifests, verified committed prefixes, exact raw replay and live/replay intent parity. Do not view labels, class frequencies, returns, model scores or comparator results during collection. Operational health and storage reports may be monitored without opening outcomes.

The future period must contain **at least 30 complete UTC days** after the artifact freeze. It must have at least **1,000 non-overlapping paired five-minute episodes** from at least 30 distinct days, at least **200 examples of each primary target class**, and at least **200 effectively non-overlapping observations in each of five predeclared probability buckets** before a calibrated probability can be published. Select non-overlapping episodes by earliest decision ordinal, then skip until the previous selected outcome exit; do not pick favorable endpoints. These are lower bounds; serial dependence across days can still invalidate inference. If class or health minima fail, the period is inconclusive, not a negative label manufactured from censors. A later additional untouched period may be collected under a new frozen protocol.

Healthy current-book/depth eligibility must cover at least **90% of calendar decision minutes** and at least **90% of eligible decisions** must pair under the frozen endpoint rules. Every reconnect, gap, checksum/clock/data-quality incident, and censor must be accounted for by ordinal and reason. No unexplained live/replay divergence or missing committed data is allowed. A provider-side silent omission beyond the Kraken checksum remains an acknowledged source limit.

## One-time statistics and economics

The **primary statistical metric** is paired mean binary log-loss improvement of the frozen V3 `depth_up_300_v1` predictor over the earlier-training-only base-rate predictor, on the identical sealed paired rows. Require improvement of at least **0.01 nats per episode** and a positive lower bound of a 95% UTC-day block bootstrap interval for the paired improvement. The interval resamples whole days (10,000 deterministic draws, seed recorded before opening). Also report Brier score, class balance, regime slices, non-overlapping and all-row versions, and the unselected V1/V2/trivial controls. A model selected or refit using sealed outcomes invalidates this test.

Calibration is separately required before any probability field is exposed: chronological development OOF calibration must already be frozen; on sealed data, five equal-count buckets based only on frozen predicted values must each have at least 200 effective non-overlapping observations; aggregate expected calibration error must be at most **0.05**, and absolute observed-minus-mean-predicted rate must be at most **0.10** in every bucket. Report Wilson intervals and UTC-day block uncertainty. A missing bucket, rare class, or unstable intervals means calibration unavailable, not a passed gate. An uncalibrated raw logistic score is never called a probability.

The **primary economic criterion** is a positive lower bound of the 95% UTC-day block bootstrap interval for mean hypothetical **net bps per deduplicated actionable long thesis**, with at least **200 such theses** across at least 30 days. Use frozen V1 side-correct USD 100 displayed-depth entry/exit, 20-bp taker fee plus 5-bp adverse allowance per side, two-second entry delay, path censoring and a fixed 10-bp uncertainty margin at decision time. Count the same health-eligible paired timestamps and costs for V1/V2, NO_TRADE, always-long, momentum, reversion and fixed random controls. Positive prediction with zero or negative net opportunity fails this economic gate. Spot bearish predictions remain nonactionable without a separately authorized and validated short mechanism.

These thresholds are conservative engineering decision rules, not power guarantees. If the policy stays `NO_TRADE`, its zero observed loss is not positive economic evidence and the 200-action gate fails. Fee sensitivities may be disclosed, but the frozen 20+5 scenario is the acceptance result.

## Opening rule

Open and evaluate the sealed outcomes **once** with the frozen scripts and artifact. Archive the complete report, raw/replay hashes, manifest, every candidate and comparator result, censor accounting, test command outputs and any failure. If the result motivates changing a target, feature, hyperparameter, calibrator, threshold, cost or code, this period becomes exposed development data. Another later unopened period is required for a new final claim. Do not repeatedly peek at this period or redefine success after observing it.

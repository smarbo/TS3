# V3 research result: exposed development tape

Closed out 2026-10-16. This is an exploratory result on the already exposed 2026-09-27/28 public Kraken BTC/USD recording, **not** a sealed test or a claim of predictive or economic value. The exact row, fold, model and comparator figures are in the [walk-forward report](V3_WALK_FORWARD_REPORT.md), [experiment ledger](V3_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md), and [machine evidence](evidence/v3/).

## Data and target

Two independent exports of all 12,316,574 committed source ordinals yielded byte-identical `v3.features.1` rows (SHA-256 `190b9e5d23ee3541f792ff4ab7e7acb2f2702028d63baeaef3b0365507303b6d`). The existing V1/V2 path generated 1,446 calendar decisions, 1,339 healthy/depth-eligible decisions and 1,302 paired five-minute outcome rows; 31 unhealthy paths, one missing entry and five run-end episodes were censored. No V3 feature removed a paired row. The primary `depth_up_300_v1` target is the sign of the delayed-entry to exit displayed-depth executable price change **before** fees and allowance: 611 positive, 691 nonpositive. The secondary frozen-cost net-positive long outcome has only three positives. There are 1,254 normal and 48 volatile paired rows, **219** earliest decision-time non-overlapping full-day episodes, and one market day. Overlapping labels and shared market conditions make the effective independent sample smaller still.

The seven frozen columns are 60-second return, 300-second displacement, 900-second RMS movement, current spread, 30-second book pressure, the accepted V1 volatile flag, and V2 signal disagreement. They are causal decision-snapshot features; training labels are constructed downstream from later quote endpoints. Exact units, availability and version contracts are in [V3_DESIGN](V3_DESIGN.md). No future outcome is used as an inference input. Each fold estimates normalization only on its earlier purged training rows.

## Models and chronological comparison

The registered search was one primary target, one feature schema, an earlier-training Laplace base-rate null and deterministic L2 logistic regression with penalties 1 and 10. Five expanding chronological folds use a six-minute decision purge plus actual label-exit barrier, two-hour validation intervals and a 30-minute later-training embargo. The folds contain 120, 100, 120, 120 and 120 paired validation rows. Their exact UTC and ordinal boundaries, coefficients, class counts, health coverage, gross/net outcomes, regime slices and comparators are preserved in the [walk-forward report](V3_WALK_FORWARD_REPORT.md). This is 580 out-of-fold rows but only 98 earliest decision-time non-overlapping episodes from 25 half-hour blocks in one day.

The primary paired mean log loss was **0.702734** for the null, **0.716927** for penalty 1 and **0.713826** for penalty 10. Corresponding Brier scores were 0.254753, 0.260584 and 0.259346. Penalty 10 was the less poor logistic model, yet lost to the null by 0.011091 nats per row and lost on three of five folds. On the 98 non-overlapping out-of-fold episodes, mean null-minus-model log-loss improvement was -0.017923. A preregistered fixed-seed half-hour block bootstrap gave a descriptive 95% range [-0.02580, +0.00594] nats. Because all blocks are from one day, that range is not a day-level generalization interval. Neither logistic fit earned promotion as a predictive candidate. The full-day penalty-10 artifact is for versioned read-only shadow inference and failure-path testing only. The post-outcome non-overlap count correction, which did not alter model fits or primary metrics, is disclosed in the [experiment ledger](V3_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md).

Six predeclared single-column penalty-10 ablations yielded aggregate log losses 0.712504 (without return), 0.709613 (without displacement), 0.717233 (without book pressure), 0.713826 (without volatile), 0.714111 (without disagreement) and 0.712817 (without spread). All lose to the null. The ablation runner retained primary log loss but omitted secondary Brier; this reporting limitation is disclosed rather than backfilled by an outcome-driven variant. Coefficients for return and displacement change sign over folds; the volatile weight is zero in every fold's training population. The 23 volatile validation rows in fold 4 are too few for a reliable regime claim. Book pressure's apparent ablation contribution is dependent, modest and not proof of a separate mechanism. The synthetic correlated-feature, future-fitted-regime, calibration-leakage and future-label fixtures test these failure modes.

No ridge return model was attempted: the net-positive long label has three positives and one dependent day cannot support an additional model search. No boosted tree, forest, neural model, threshold sweep or additional target was attempted. The complete tried-family count, including rejected and conditional families, remains in the ledger.

## Calibration, costs and decisions

Only 98 non-overlapping out-of-fold validation episodes from one day are available. They fail the predeclared calibration minimum of 30 days, 1,000 non-overlapping episodes, 200 per primary class and five buckets with at least 200 effective observations each. No calibrator, reliability bucket, expected calibration error, calibrated probability or probability-based decision threshold is asserted. The logistic sigmoid appears only as an **uncalibrated diagnostic** for log loss and Brier; live/replay V3 intents omit a probability and expected return. The artifact binds its training run and latest fitted-label exit, so historical in-sample replay cannot emit retroactive model scores.

V1, V2 and V3 policies and `NO_TRADE` made zero directional actions on the identical 580-row out-of-fold comparison population. Under the frozen 20-bp taker fee plus 5-bp allowance per side, always-long made 580 hypothetical actions and averaged -49.020 net bps per action; fixed random direction made 580 and lost -29,640.38 bps total; trivial momentum made 37 and lost -1,826.41 bps total; trivial reversion made 79 and lost -3,990.15 bps total. On all 1,302 pairs, the predeclared always-long sensitivity means were -30.719, -50.718 and -170.714 net bps at 10+5, 20+5 and 80+5 bps per side, with 26, three and zero positive outcomes. These are side-correct displayed-depth and assumed-fee scenarios, not fills or account-specific prices. The [cost review](V3_COST_REVIEW.md) records the public fee-table check made before inspecting V3 outcomes. No cost scenario is selected for a favorable return.

V3 has no validated mapping from an uncalibrated direction score to positive net opportunity after spread, fees, allowance and uncertainty margin. Bearish evidence is not an executable spot short. `NO_TRADE` is the only justified shadow intent. Zero trading loss from abstention is **not** evidence of a profitable strategy.

## Acceptance boundary and next evidence

The exposed day supports engineering reproducibility and rejection of weak hypotheses. It does not validate calibration, economic value or a frozen production candidate. A later **development** chronology must first support a prospective multi-day OOF schedule and any calibration. Only after code, artifact, costs and decision policy are frozen can a different, unopened **sealed** period be collected and opened once. The [sealed-test protocol](V3_SEALED_TEST_PROTOCOL.md) requires at least 30 complete UTC days, 1,000 non-overlapping paired episodes, 200 examples of each primary class and five buckets of at least 200 effective observations for probability publication, plus health, paired statistical improvement and economic gates. Its current one-day artifact cannot simply be called the candidate for that future test. No V4 or account/order functionality follows from this result.

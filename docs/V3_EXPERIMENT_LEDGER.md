**Post-outcome audit correction, 2026-10-16:** The initial `Nonoverlap` helper selected a later decision if its delayed *entry* occurred after the prior selected exit, even when the decision itself preceded that exit. That overstated the full-day descriptive effective count as 226 rather than 219 and OOF count as 103 rather than 98. The corrected helper and independent Python audit both use decision time, matching the frozen sealed protocol; a regression fixture covers a delayed entry after a preceding exit. This did not change the exported rows, training/validation memberships, labels, normalization, fit, log loss, Brier, costs, policy or shadow inference. It did change the descriptive OOF mean from -0.024129 to -0.017923 and the artifact hash through its honest training-revision field. The correction was made after seeing outcomes and is disclosed; it does not promote any model or relax any gate.

The ablation loop recorded its preregistered log-loss metric but omitted ablation Brier values. This reporting limit does not affect the primary null comparison or model selection; the shared 580-row cohort's descriptive gross/net outcome remains +1.088/-49.020 bps per row under the frozen costs for every ablation. A later variant must prospectively register any additional secondary ablation metric before using it for selection.

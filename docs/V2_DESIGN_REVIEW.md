# Adversarial review of proposed V2 core

Reviewed before implementation or V2 outcome inspection, 2026-09-30. Each correction below is incorporated in [V2_DESIGN](V2_DESIGN.md).

1. **HIGH — Three family names could disguise two correlated price views. ACCEPT.** Trend and reversion share V1 midpoint samples and the same five-minute outcome. They occupy one price slot; agreement does not double voting weight. Book pressure is a separate displayed-liquidity mechanism, with a conditional ablation required before claiming value.
2. **HIGH — A 30-second book signal could be misrepresented as a five-minute forecast. ACCEPT.** Its role is confirmation only. The output records its short horizon; a price-family thesis and pressure agreement are both required. Historical conditional skill must be shown rather than assumed.
3. **HIGH — Zero imbalance may conceal stale or absent depth. ACCEPT.** `NEUTRAL` is reserved for a healthy, warmed, current quote with observed pressure inside the trigger. Missing/invalid/wide/stale/insufficient samples become `UNAVAILABLE` or `INVALID`, and the consensus gate cannot silently substitute zero.
4. **HIGH — Aggregating uncalibrated signed scores as expected edge would manufacture a probability. ACCEPT.** The aggregator votes by independent mechanism slot. The cost screen uses a clearly labelled past-move proxy, not predicted return. It never emits calibrated probability or expected net return; positive actions are research hypotheses only.
5. **HIGH — Reusing V1 code but mutating its output would move the benchmark. ACCEPT.** V2 owns an unchanged V1 engine instance and copies its minute snapshot. V1 default config, journal schema, research report and archived hashes remain reproducible. Any V2 output has a new schema and digest.
6. **HIGH — A book update before the delayed entry boundary could be selected by replay. ACCEPT.** The downstream evaluator requires both the tick and the book update available after the two-second boundary, with the same side-correct V1 cost function and path censoring. The decision engine receives no evaluator handle.
7. **HIGH — Repeated minute actions would overstate independent opportunities. ACCEPT.** Report raw decisions separately from downstream deduplicated theses. A five-minute thesis horizon and explicit same-direction/reversal rule prevent counting every repeated minute as an independent trade.
8. **HIGH — A second feed would need a global admission order and matching historical tape. ACCEPT.** No second feed is admitted in core V2. Optional derivatives/cross-market/trades are postponed rather than joined by exchange timestamp or silently backfilled. The existing accepted tape remains sufficient.
9. **MEDIUM — Pressure could overweight unchanged quotes. ACCEPT.** Sample only when `LastBookOrdinal` advances; reset on quality/generation loss; require ten distinct updates over at least 20 seconds. Persist the contributing ordinal.
10. **MEDIUM — A favorable result on the exposed one-day tape could invite threshold selection. ACCEPT.** The ledger freezes all thresholds and parameter variants before replay. Four folds and block intervals are descriptive development analysis, not a sealed holdout. No performance-based policy revision is accepted as fresh validation on this tape.

No unresolved CRITICAL or HIGH design ambiguity remains for the single-feed core. The design does not claim that these mechanisms are statistically independent or profitable. Those claims require later untouched chronology.

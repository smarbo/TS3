# Adversarial V3 design review

Reviewed against the V3-001 design before model training, 2026-10-02. Findings below are resolved in [V3_DESIGN](V3_DESIGN.md) unless marked as a data limit.

1. **CRITICAL — exposed V0/V2 tape masquerading as a final test: ACCEPT.** It is development data. A separate future period is opened once only after code, artifact, target, costs and gates are frozen. Redesign after opening consumes the period.
2. **CRITICAL — overlapping labels leak across fold boundaries: ACCEPT.** The exporter records actual entry/exit times; training rows whose exit touches validation are purged, plus a six-minute decision gap. A 30-minute post-validation embargo applies when those rows could enter later training. Tests include boundary equality and delayed endpoints.
3. **HIGH — global normalization or future-fit regime thresholds: ACCEPT.** Scalers are fit solely on training rows inside each fold. The accepted V1 regime uses fixed, unfitted thresholds; any learned regime threshold must be fold-local. A future-fitted synthetic fixture must fail the protocol.
4. **HIGH — three net-positive longs cannot support cost-clearing calibration: ACCEPT.** The primary diagnostic label is executable *price component* direction before fees; net-positive remains descriptive. Neither label justifies a calibrated probability on this tape. No probability goes into intents.
5. **HIGH — a direction classifier could be misread as positive expected value: ACCEPT.** The model-driven policy remains `NO_TRADE` unless independently validated return magnitude clears side-correct costs plus uncertainty. Spot short remains unavailable.
6. **HIGH — V2 family names hide correlated transformations: ACCEPT.** Seven small columns include one price-return and one displacement view; ablations and correlation disclosure are mandatory. No correlated-feature coefficient is called causal importance.
7. **HIGH — comparing models on different health populations or costs: ACCEPT.** Freeze the V1 paired population; attach V2 by ordinal; compare all controls on identical endpoints. Health, censoring, regimes and cost assumptions are reported first.
8. **HIGH — in-sample Platt calibration looks convincing: ACCEPT.** Only earlier chronological OOF predictions can train a calibrator; later OOF predictions evaluate it. Minimum effective bucket and class counts rule out calibration here. A synthetic leakage fixture must distinguish the two.
9. **HIGH — tiny effective sample and rare volatile regime: ACCEPT as an evidence limit.** About 260 optimistic non-overlapping episodes and at most ten volatile ones permit plumbing tests, not reliable regime-specific calibration or a boosted-tree search. Trees are postponed.
10. **HIGH — hyperparameter and ablation peeking inflates the winning score: ACCEPT.** Only two fixed logistic penalties and a null model enter primary selection. All six ablations and any ridge diagnostic are disclosed as exploratory; a winning development variant needs new sealed chronology.
11. **HIGH — artifact hash alone cannot prove inference parity: ACCEPT.** Validate the schema, coefficient order, training boundary and config in Go; reject corrupt/nonfinite/out-of-range artifacts. Repeat the same raw replay and compare V3 canonical intent hashes; live path uses the same engine.
12. **MEDIUM — assumed 20-bp fees may differ from a later account: ACCEPT as scope limit.** Keep the accepted frozen cost scenario for paired comparison. Any externally documented alternative is labeled sensitivity, never used to pick a favorable result. No current fee/account claim is made.

No CRITICAL/HIGH design weakness is left unaddressed. Engineering implementation and later evidence can still expose new defects. The final statistical gate is knowingly blocked by absent sealed chronology.

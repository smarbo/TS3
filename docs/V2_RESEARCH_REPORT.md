## Method

The V2 core uses trend persistence and local displacement reversion as one price mechanism, plus 30-second persistent top-five displayed book-depth imbalance as a distinct confirming mechanism. Each family reports `ACTIVE`, `NEUTRAL`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or `INVALID`; price and book must vote in agreement. The cost screen uses the same USD 100 depth sweep, 20-bp taker fee and 5-bp allowance per side, plus a fixed 10-bp margin. Its observed past-move proxy is **not** expected gross return. Default spot configuration lacks a short mechanism, so a bearish consensus remains `NO_TRADE` even if the proxy screen passes. No weights, thresholds or outcomes were fitted on this tape.

One-minute decisions are overlapping observations. The five-minute paired research uses V1's unchanged first fresh post-two-second entry, first fresh post-entry-plus-300-second exit and path-health/depth censoring. A separate matched 30-second endpoint study tests book pressure at its declared horizon. Report health/calendar coverage and all censored episodes before interpreting returns. The five-minute price/book cohorts treat pressure as confirmation; they do not establish standalone 30-second skill. Raw directional intents and research-only deduplicated theses are separate counts. Four chronological development folds and 30-minute-block bootstrap intervals are descriptive; they do not create independent samples or a final holdout.

## Hypothetical outcomes, controls and limitations

All results below are *displayed-depth quote scenarios*, not fills. Gross return is midpoint movement; friction includes side-correct depth, the fixed 20-bp taker fee and 5-bp adverse allowance per side, and short borrow where applicable. Five-minute episodes overlap by design, so action counts are not independent trades.

- On the five-minute paired population, unconstrained V2 trend direction took 53 hypothetical actions: mean gross **+3.66 bps**, friction **50.18 bps**, net **-46.51 bps** per action, zero positive net actions. Reversion took 177: mean gross **-0.92**, friction **50.15**, net **-51.07 bps**, zero positive net actions. Book pressure used as *five-minute confirmation only* took 1,050: mean gross **+1.09**, friction **50.13**, net **-49.03 bps** per action, three positive net actions. That last row is not a standalone five-minute book-signal skill claim.
- On its declared **30-second** endpoint, book pressure took 1,070 hypothetical actions from 1,332 paired minutes: mean gross **+0.28 bps**, friction **50.10 bps**, net **-49.82 bps** per action, zero positive net actions. A direction-randomized control matched to its 1,070 active timestamps averaged **+0.14 gross / -49.96 net bps** per action; always-long averaged **+0.04 gross / -50.06 net bps**. The small gross difference against random is descriptive on dependent one-day observations and nowhere near the assumed costs. V1 and V2 policies and no-trade controls all took zero actions on this same 30-second population.
- On the 31 consensus minutes, following the price vote *without* the cost veto would have averaged **+4.63 gross / 50.22 friction / -45.60 net bps** per action, with zero positive net actions. The 96 price-vote hypothetical actions in the disagreement cohort averaged **-2.27 gross / -52.39 net bps**; this does not prove that disagreement predicts losses on future data. The 45 paired trend/book-agreement minutes include only 13 price-vote hypothetical actions because the other price family can conflict; those 13 averaged **+3.53 gross / -46.74 net bps**. The 23 reversion-alone hypothetical actions averaged **+4.57 gross / -45.60 net bps**. These overlapping cohorts are not separate trade populations.
- The paired price-vote hypothetical was negative in both observed regimes: 133 actions in `NORMAL` averaged **-50.68 net bps/action**, and 17 in `VOLATILE` averaged **-44.23**. These regime slices overlap the family and conflict cohorts above; 48 paired volatile minutes are too few for a robust regime conclusion. There was no `WIDE` decision to evaluate empirically.
- The frozen V1 policy also took zero actions. Its unchanged 90-action momentum and 185-action reversion comparators averaged about **-48.94** and **-50.49 net bps** per action; always-long averaged **-50.72**. The V2 audit matched their exact archived paired/censored population and comparator sums. Neither baseline has a validated positive expected value. The V2 policy's alternative 0/10-bp *outcome* allowance summaries remain zero because it took no actions; they are not a policy threshold sweep or evidence that a cheaper fee would create a valid action.

The public tape was exposed before V2-001 was written. No trigger, parameter, cost, weight or optional source was selected from these V2 outcomes, and no sealed period or forward economic test exists. The 30-minute-block intervals and fold slices are descriptive and cannot make overlapping minute decisions independent. Favorable/adverse *policy trade* excursion is undefined because the policy took no actions; this report does not impute executable path extrema from midpoint quotes. The `SHORT` signal is visible for research, but spot short execution feasibility was never established. Quote liquidity can disappear, assumed fees are not a current account fee schedule, and Kraken's book channel has no contiguous source sequence guarantee. The broad negative net results are evidence against calling these exploratory families tradeable under the frozen cost scenario, not evidence that the policy is profitable.

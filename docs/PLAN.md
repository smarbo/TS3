# Delivery plan and evidence gates

Milestones are sequential. Scope changes require updating [SPEC](SPEC.md), [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md), [RESEARCH](RESEARCH.md), and [STATUS](STATUS.md) where affected. Passing a milestone's engineering tests does not establish a trading edge. Versions and thresholds are frozen before each evaluation period.

## V0 — Data collection and deterministic replay

**Exact scope.** Implement only public Coinbase Exchange `BTC-USD` `level2` and `heartbeat` over the unauthenticated market-data WebSocket, plus one public product-metadata GET before the socket opens. A single ingress coordinator orders observed frames, metadata, ticks, and lifecycle barriers. Record them with UTC receive/admission/usable times and ordinals, normalize to `BookSnapshot`, `BookDelta`, `Heartbeat`, `ProductMetadata`, `SourceStatus`, and `ClockTick`, maintain the Level 2 book and quality states, and replay the **committed** raw tape through the same normalizer/quality/book path. The manifest indexes ordered metadata but cannot supply it directly to state. Include the monotonic live availability gate and progress high-water marks. Exact contracts are in [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md). No analytical signal or intent is required in V0.

**Explicit non-scope.** No account keys, private APIs, order management, trade matches, REST history backfill, L3, candles, multi-product join, feature engineering, regime/signal/decision models, or paper P&L. No REST book bootstrap because Level 2 emits its own snapshot.

**Deliverables.** Go module; `cmd/collector` and `cmd/replay`; single ingress/epoch coordinator; versioned raw segment writer/reader with CRC32C, SHA-256 prefix commit frames and recovery; canonical normalized JSONL exporter; Coinbase public adapter; single-owner quality/book state; monotonic availability watchdog; applied/published progress journal and recovery report; metrics and structured logs; synthetic `testdata` tapes and expected hashes; runbook with capture/replay and incident recovery commands. A run manifest records run ID, source endpoint, product/channel, start/end, code revision, Go version, config/schema hashes, segment hashes, ordered metadata reference, high-water marks, and clean/unclean termination. It is not an analytical input.

**Clock, recording and replay rules.** Live records monotonic one-second tick observations through the same serialized ingress as frames. `ReceiveTime` is stamped after complete frame read, `AdmissionTime` when ingress accepts it, and `UsableFromTime` no earlier than admission. A read-completed frame overtaken by an admitted tick remains later by ordinal. `EventTime` is null for `snapshot`, source `time` for `l2update` and heartbeat. Never sort by event time. A wall/monotonic elapsed discrepancy over two seconds ends the run with `CLOCK_ANOMALY`; a new run needs ten stable one-second observations and a new snapshot. Replay reads only records through verified commit frames in ordinal order, uses recorded ticks/times, and makes no network or OS-clock calls in analytical code. Speed cannot change canonical state hashes. Recovery distinguishes structurally valid bytes, committed prefix, applied prefix, and published prefix; it never includes an uncommitted suffix.

**Quality and book rules.** Start `STARTING`; require an ordered online product response, verified acknowledgment listing exactly both configured channel/product pairs, current-epoch snapshot, and heartbeat before `HEALTHY`. Reject wrong-product frames and duplicate/mismatched acknowledgments. A valid snapshot atomically creates a book generation only after whole-message validation, including unique canonical prices, positive sizes, bounded decimals and at most 250,000 levels per side. Prevalidate each entire delta, then apply absolute sizes in message order; zero removes a level. Disconnect, parser/integrity error, over-three-second analytical heartbeat age, crossed/empty book, or wrong epoch invalidates the book. Require acknowledgment within ten seconds and snapshot within ten more; retry source failures with one-to-30-second exponential backoff plus ±20% jitter, a new epoch barrier, and new snapshot. Recorder/storage failure terminates the run. A 30-second quiet book is `DEGRADED`, not proof of loss. Heartbeat sequence gaps do not fabricate Level 2 deltas. The independent operational gate closes on excessive live progress lag even before the analytical tick is applied.

**Initial metrics.** Frames/bytes per type, unknown/invalid/wrong-product frames, ingress and recorder queue depth/blocked time, fsync latency, commit/applied/published high-water marks, receive-to-admit/commit/apply/publish lag, tick-to-state and gate-close lag, epoch/retry counts, heartbeat and quote age, snapshot wait, book generation/levels/spread/crossed/empty incidents, clock anomalies, time unhealthy/degraded, and replay prefix/hash mismatches. Metric labels may include the one configured instrument and source, never unbounded raw IDs.

**Exact acceptance tests.** The implementation agent must provide fixtures and commands that demonstrate all of the following:

1. **Identity/protocol:** public metadata GET is admitted before WebSocket open. Outbound subscribe contains exactly `BTC-USD`, `level2`, `heartbeat`. Missing/extra channel or product, duplicate acknowledgment, wrong/missing-product heartbeat/snapshot/delta, and snapshot before acknowledgment never reach `HEALTHY` or mutate the BTC book. A future unknown type stays raw and is ignored without invalidating a valid epoch.
2. **Capture order/epoch:** pause handoff after a frame read while a tick and disconnect occur; verify admission ordinal governs as-of state despite earlier `ReceiveTime`. Delay an old-epoch delta/close while new connection is requested; the coordinator must commit the old close barrier before authorizing new connection, and stale old-epoch status cannot invalidate the new book. Assert state after every ordinal.
3. **Raw commit/recovery:** verify exact frame bytes, length/CRC, ordinal continuity, prefix/segment hashes, commit-marker digest, manifest, normalized JSONL and decimals. Kill at write, data fsync, marker write/fsync, state apply, and output write. Recovery reports valid bytes, committed, acknowledged applied, and acknowledged published prefixes plus uncertain/uncommitted suffixes. A complete unmarked frame is excluded; missing final manifest is rebuilt; bit flip fails closed.
4. **Book golden:** multiple sorted/unsorted levels; canonical duplicate prices (including conflicting sizes), zero-size snapshot level, over-scale/over-magnitude decimal, more than 250,000 levels, malformed multi-change delta, replacement, zero deletion, absent-level zero, second snapshot, pre-snapshot and old-epoch deltas, empty/crossed book. Invalid whole messages change no book bytes and never leave the generation healthy. Verify sorted book hash, BBO, generation and health at each ordinal.
5. **Time/availability:** out-of-order exchange times, equal receipt stamps, no snapshot event time, a frame read before but admitted after a tick, late market frame, and poison future metadata. Verify nondecreasing usable time, ordinal-only admission, no prior output change and no manifest side-input access. Inject +1-hour jump/correction and eleven normal observations; old run remains unusable, new run starts only after ten stable observations with fresh metadata/snapshot. Compare heartbeat/quote ages across boundary.
6. **Health and backlog:** heartbeat expires after over three recorded seconds; quiet book over 30 seconds with live heartbeat becomes `DEGRADED`. Freeze recorder or state over two seconds while socket stays connected; operational gate closes by the two-second stale-progress bound plus 250 ms watchdog check, even if last analytical health says `HEALTHY`. Verify incident record when storage permits and gap-marked new run when it does not. One-second handoff/commit deadlines fail closed; no stale cached output escapes.
7. **Lifecycle:** transport disconnect, acknowledgment/snapshot timeout, malformed required frame, slow consumer, disk-full, SIGINT/SIGTERM. Verify bounded retries only for recoverable source faults, no invisible drops, fatal storage exit, orderly close barrier, committed prefix and all progress marks, and next-run gap after an incomplete run.
8. **Determinism and parity:** fast, paced, and stepped replay of the same committed tape yield identical normalized, quality, and book hashes. Compare live with replay on the **common acknowledged applied prefix**; separately enumerate committed but unapplied/unpublished suffixes and operational gate intervals. Replay speed must not rewrite measured live apply/publication timestamps.
9. **Information barrier:** a probe receiving one admitted event at a time cannot query future records, final manifest, or replay reader. Poison later frames/metadata and verify every earlier output and contributing-ordinal list remains unchanged.
10. **Soak:** capture at least 24 continuous hours of configured feed and report frame/byte/storage rates, p50/p99 and maximum admission/commit/apply/tick lag, fsync latency, queue maxima, high-water marks, time unhealthy/degraded, prolonged quiet-book intervals, clock adjustments/UTC offset if measured, all reconnect/gap/invalid events, and exact live/replay comparison prefix. Pass with **zero unreported loss or unexplained common-prefix divergence**; disconnections are permitted only if marked and resnapshotted. This proves engineering behavior for that run, not exchange-side completeness or strategy value. If unavailable, V0 remains open.

**Gate to V1.** All tests above pass; the 24-hour capture manifest, committed-prefix replay, common applied-prefix comparison, and any suffix/gate incident report are archived; remaining quality limitations (especially silent provider-side omissions) are documented; no prohibited connectivity exists. Do not substitute a synthetic tape for the soak.

## V1 — Deterministic baseline analytical engine

**Scope.** Add the single shared `AnalysisEngine`, midpoint return/volatility/trend and spread/depth features, a simple documented regime summary, one or two baseline signal rules, a conservative quote-aware hypothetical cost model at a predeclared reference notional, and `LONG`/`SHORT`/`NO_TRADE` intents. Use only V0 feeds and explicit fixed-horizon labels. A bearish forecast yields `NO_TRADE` unless a hypothetical short mechanism and borrow/carry costs are specified. Add a run manifest for feature versions, assumptions, and intent provenance. Emit probabilities only after chronological out-of-fold, purged, predeclared calibration validation; otherwise omit those fields.

**Non-scope.** No trade-flow, derivatives, cross-market signals, ML, execution, leverage/size, or optimized parameter sweeps. No claim of economic value from software correctness.

**Deliverables and tests.** Pure incremental feature/state packages; baseline and cost definitions; intent schema/journal with measured live decision-ready/publication times; fixture tests for warm-up, missing/stale book, quote generation and maximum age, as-of bar revisions, late events, `NO_TRADE` reasons, cost-margin gate, and common-prefix output parity. Before any return result, freeze a reference notional, earliest-entry latency rule, first eligible healthy **ask for buy/bid for sell** and opposite-side exit rule, depth consumption/slippage, round-trip fees, horizon measured from feasible entry, borrow/carry where relevant, and absent-endpoint censoring; no favorable earlier quote may be selected. A recorder/output stall across a price jump and a hand-calculated moving-BBO/thin-depth/missing-exit tape must prove the evaluator cannot backdate entry. Report midpoint prediction skill separately from net hypothetical opportunity. Chronological evaluation includes `NO_TRADE`, random direction at matched action frequency, always-long where meaningful, simple momentum and reversion under identical costs. Freeze a health eligibility mask and report calendar coverage, reason counts, and outage-driven versus model-driven abstention. Report excluded-period moves/volatility only if independently observed; otherwise mark unknown. Compare baselines on paired eligible timestamps; an outage clustered around adverse moves must appear as lost coverage, not improved model skill. Test leakage with poisoned future values and shuffled event-time order.

**Acceptance/evidence gate.** Engineering invariants pass; the experiment ledger and evaluation protocol are frozen and report uncertainty, trade frequency, coverage, regime breakdown and costs. V1/V2 comparisons use reusable chronological **development** walk-forward folds, not the sealed final period. Proceed to V2 only if baseline behavior and limitations are understood and enough clean data exist; positive returns are not mandatory and cannot be manufactured by retuning an exposed final period.

## V2 — Multi-signal engine

**Scope.** Add public matches/trades only after a timestamp/gap/backfill design review; implement at most a small number of mechanism-distinct signal families (for example trend, mean reversion, signed-flow). Add family health declarations, disagreement measurement, a simple dependence-aware ensemble, and optional one additional instrument/context source only if its incremental hypothesis is registered. Add available-time-safe joining for any second feed.

**Non-scope.** No large indicator catalogue, complex ML, account/execution features, or unverified alternative data.

**Deliverables and tests.** Trade capture and reconciliation design/implementation if approved by evidence; versioned feature/signal contracts; missing-feed and stale-join tests; ablation and family-correlation reports; paired chronological development walk-forward comparisons against V1 under identical costs/latency. Gap backfill must preserve fetch receipt time and may not revise old decisions. Before admitting any second feed, specify one global admission order or deterministic coordinator with per-feed arrival ordinals, decision cutoffs, bounded wait, stale/missing outcomes and no retroactive decisions; a delayed second-feed fixture must match live in fast and paced replay.

**Acceptance/evidence gate.** Additional feeds are reconstructible in replay; independent-family claims are supported by correlation/ablation evidence or withdrawn; complexity earns an out-of-sample benefit robust to costs and regimes, or is removed. Failure to beat V1 is a valid result.

## V3 — Statistical / ML modelling

**Scope.** Only if V1/V2 data and sample size justify it, compare regularized linear/logistic models and then perhaps boosted trees with explicitly versioned feature sets, training windows, purging/embargo for overlapping labels, calibration, and uncertainty. Maintain the same engine and availability barrier.

**Non-scope.** No deep models by default, online learning without a frozen evaluation protocol, training on final test, or using final test for model selection.

**Deliverables and tests.** Reproducible training pipeline, model artifact/hash, time-split and walk-forward reports, calibration/reliability plots, sensitivity and parameter-stability checks, complete experiment/search ledger, leakage probes and ablations. Generate calibrator inputs from chronological out-of-fold predictions with purge/embargo for overlapping outcome horizons; fit regime thresholds and family selection inside each fold. Predeclare effective independent episode count, bucket uncertainty, and an acceptance metric before populating probability fields. A synthetic correlated-family/future-fitted-regime fixture must reject in-sample calibration. Compare to all earlier baselines after identical costs.

**Acceptance/evidence gate.** Development out-of-sample incremental performance and calibration survive walk-forward, regime and cost sensitivity with dependence-aware uncertainty reported; otherwise retain simpler models or `NO_TRADE`. Open the sealed final period once for a frozen candidate. If its result prompts a redesign, that exposed period becomes development evidence and new future data are required for another final test.

## V4 — Forward paper validation

**Scope.** Run the frozen analysis engine prospectively on recorded public feeds and publish intents to an append-only research journal. Evaluate subsequent market outcomes with predeclared label and hypothetical friction rules. Track availability, latency, drift, calibration and abstention.

**Non-scope.** No real orders, positions, account APIs, wallets, leverage selection, or unattended execution. Paper observations are not fills.

**Deliverables and tests.** Signed/frozen run manifest, continuous raw capture, immutable intents, outcome evaluator that cannot edit earlier intents, monitoring and incident log. Replay reproduces canonical intent decisions through the common acknowledged applied prefix; original measured publication timestamps and operational gate intervals come from the live journal, not replay speed. Unpublished or unapplied suffixes are reported separately; outages yield reasoned `NO_TRADE` or unavailable output. Forward metrics include sample sizes, coverage, costs, regime breakdown and uncertainty.

**Acceptance/evidence gate.** Sufficient predeclared forward duration and sample count (set before V4 begins), no unexplained live/replay divergence or unmarked data gaps, and transparent comparison to V1/V2/V3 baselines. This milestone ends at research evidence; any execution system would require a separate project and authorization.

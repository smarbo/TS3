## Attempts to disprove the model claim

1. **Future information and temporal contamination.** Five expanding folds use a six-minute decision purge, require every fitted label's actual exit strictly before validation starts, and exclude each prior validation's following 30-minute embargo from later training. The independent audit reconstructs membership from row bytes, not from the trainer's claimed counts. Standardization is fitted on each fold's training rows only. The accepted V1 regime threshold is fixed. Synthetic future-label, future-fitted-regime and calibration-in-sample fixtures reject invalid evidence. Offline artifact inference also refuses a score for every decision from its training run and for any decision at or before the latest fitted-label exit. This closes the same-run backdating path found before V3 outcomes were opened.
2. **Selection and parameter instability.** One target, seven columns, a base-rate null and two registered logistic penalties were the primary search. Both logistic fits lose to the null on identical 580 out-of-fold rows; penalty 10 loses on three of five folds, and its price-feature coefficients change sign. Six disclosed ablations all lose to the null. No ridge, tree, threshold or cost sweep was promoted after inspecting outcomes. The apparent book-pressure ablation effect is not independent evidence of a mechanism. The omitted ablation Brier values are a disclosed secondary-reporting limit; the primary comparison is intact.
3. **Dependence and regime coverage.** The final conservative decision-time non-overlap count is 219 full-day episodes, only 98 in the five out-of-fold windows, from one market day. Fold-4's volatile validation cohort has 23 overlapping rows and only a handful of distinct episodes. Neither row count nor a 25-block half-hour bootstrap supplies a reliable cross-day uncertainty interval. The descriptive OOF non-overlap mean null-minus-model log-loss improvement is -0.017923; the all-row half-hour interval [-0.02580, +0.00594] crosses zero. A positive edge cannot be inferred.
4. **Post-outcome count correction.** The initial descriptive helper used delayed entry time for overlap selection, allowing seven extra full-day and five extra OOF decisions made before a previous selected outcome exited. That conflicted with the predeclared sealed protocol. Commit `f6b1825` changed the helper and independent Python audit to decision time and added a delayed-entry regression fixture. Counts moved 226 to 219 and 103 to 98. It did not alter rows, folds, model parameters, log loss, Brier, cost arithmetic or policy. The correction is disclosed in the [ledger](V3_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md) and is not used to rescue a model claim.
5. **Baseline and cost fairness.** The V1, V2, V3, `NO_TRADE`, always-long, trivial momentum/reversion and fixed random controls use the same five validation cohorts and frozen 20+5 bp per-side cost scenario. All three policies abstain; zero trading loss is not positive value. Always-long loses -49.020 net bps per action on the 580 OOF rows. On all 1,302 pairs, preregistered 10+5, 20+5 and 80+5 sensitivities are all negative. No public fee table or displayed-depth quote proves actual fills or a user's account tier.
6. **Calibration and economic mapping.** The 98 effective OOF episodes from one day fail the predeclared 30-date, 1,000-episode, 200-per-class, five-bucket count gate. A synthetic fixture across 30 distinct UTC dates with 1,020 non-overlapping episodes verifies that the count gate can open and fit an earlier-OOF Platt model before a later evaluation boundary; separate leakage fixtures reject future/in-sample calibration. This fixture does not stand in for 30 complete sealed days. No calibrator, calibrated probability or expected return is published from the actual tape. Directional raw logits have no validated return-magnitude or cost-clearing mapping, and the public spot feed supplies no actionable short. The only justified V3 intent is `NO_TRADE`.

# V3 cost reality check

Reviewed against [Kraken's published Pro spot fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule) on 2026-10-02, before V3 model outcomes. This is a public fee-table comparison, not a claim about any account, trade, or future fill.

The frozen V1/V2 research scenario assumes a **20-bp taker fee per side**, **5-bp adverse allowance per side**, and a side-correct displayed-depth sweep for a USD 100 hypothetical quantity. Its roughly 50-bp round-trip friction is mechanically consistent with those assumptions. It is **not a generic Kraken entry-tier fee**. The published table lists 80 bp per side at Tier 1 and 20 bp per side at Tier 8, the latter requiring a stated volume/assets threshold. This repository has no account connectivity and cannot establish the user's tier, an actual order route, a future schedule, or fills. The 5-bp allowance is a research assumption, not a venue quote. Displayed liquidity can vanish.

For paired V1/V2/V3 comparisons, **retain 20+5 bp per side as the frozen primary research scenario**. Do not change labels or select a fee because it produces a favorable model. Predeclared sensitivity scenarios for future V3 research are 10+5, 20+5, and 80+5 bp per side, all with the same displayed-depth endpoint and action population. Ten bp corresponds to a high published Pro tier, while 80 bp represents the published Tier 1 taker rate as checked above. Sensitivities are descriptive; the 20-bp frozen result remains the comparison benchmark and no scenario proves executable profit. Before any later sealed economic claim, recheck the contemporaneous public schedule and predeclare an applicable hypothetical account tier **before** opening outcomes. A changed fee due to independently documented venue terms needs a new prospective protocol, never a retrospective search for the best P&L.
